🌟 What We Do

We publish practical guides on clinic selection, recovery stays, visas, and language support, and map clinics from official Korea Tourism Organization (KTO) medical-tourism listings. Use our filters to explore Seoul, Busan, and other regions — then confirm every detail directly with your chosen clinic before booking.

⚠️ Important

{{ site.site_name }} is an independent information site, not a hospital or booking agency. We do not verify prices, medical outcomes, or doctor credentials beyond public listing data. Read our medical & travel disclaimer and terms of use.

✏️ Editorial standards

Guides are written for international visitors planning care in Korea. Clinic map data comes from Korea Tourism Organization (KTO) medical-tourism OpenAPI listings. We label directory-only pages and exclude thin listings from search indexing where appropriate. Spotted an error? Send a correction.

🔗 Affiliate disclosure

Some pages include partner links (for example stay, eSIM, or tour bookings) and banner ads. If you book through these links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This helps fund free guides and maps. See our Privacy Policy for details. We are not owned by clinics or booking platforms listed on the Site.
